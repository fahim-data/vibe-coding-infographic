Vibe coding is a term coined by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy in early 2025. The idea: describe what you want in plain language and let AI write the code. Instead of manually writing every line, you direct, review, and iterate. The AI does the heavy lifting. Which AI — and which kind of tool — depends almost entirely on what you're already capable of doing.
